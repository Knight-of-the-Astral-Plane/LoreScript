Blueprint

Your content shouldn't be trapped inside an app.

LoreScript can export your custom content as a Blueprint JSON file. Blueprint is designed to make game content portable, editable and reproducible. Keep it as a backup, modify it yourself, share it with friends or use other tools to work with it.

And if filling out everything manually sounds like work: give the Blueprint to an AI and let it help you.
